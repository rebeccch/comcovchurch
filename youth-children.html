Children & Youth

Our Children’s Ministry Director is Dawn Pothos, a much-loved Pre-School teacher in the community.

Sunday Mornings

Dawn provides a short family reflection during the worship service and then leads our Children’s Church program for children pre-K through 4th grade. Children remain in the sanctuary for the first 15 minutes of the service, then spend the rest of the hour with Dawn engaged in Bible stories, crafts, and other fun activities. All are welcome!

Beyond Sunday

  • Confirmation Classes for students in grades five through seven
  • Vacation Bible School (VBS) — held annually for children pre-K through 6th grade

Vacation Bible School: Rainforest Falls

Registration is open for this summer’s VBS, August 10–14, Monday through Friday from 9:30 AM to 12:00 PM. Games, activities, skits, Bible stories, and great songs — discovering the nature of God as our loving Creator.
