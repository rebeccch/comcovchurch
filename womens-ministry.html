Women's Ministry

Our Women’s Ministry exists to gather women across generations to grow in Christ together, study His word, and serve our community and the world.

Our Mission

  1. We REACH out to all generations of women and invite each one to join us on our journey.
  2. We TEACH women God’s word in our studies, and it is the foundation of all that we do.
  3. We EQUIP women to share Christ with others and lead them to Him.
  4. We SEND women out to share the Gospel and to serve others as we live out His commandments.

We welcome each one to join us as we intentionally connect and serve Christ together.
